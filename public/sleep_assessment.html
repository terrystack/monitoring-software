Putting it in context

ISS astronauts had an 8.5-hour sleep opportunity and slept 6.5 h on average (±1.4). They slept the scheduled time on only 5.9% of nights, and 34.9% of nights were 6 h or less. They slept more on weekends (6.9 h versus 6.3 h).

Limitations: observational study of 24 astronauts (5 women) with self-reported sleep. It shows associations, not causes. Your browser adds delay to reaction times, so compare your results with yourself on different days, not with the paper.
